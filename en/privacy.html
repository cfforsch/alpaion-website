2. Principles and legal bases

We only process personal data where this is necessary to operate this website, answer enquiries and provide our services, or where you have given consent. The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and, where applicable, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and national data protection laws apply.

We rely in particular on the following legal bases:

  • consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), e.g. for analytics cookies;
  • contract and pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), e.g. enquiries about our services;
  • legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), e.g. retention duties;
  • legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), e.g. the secure and stable operation of this website.

Access to information on your device (e.g. cookies) is additionally governed by the ePrivacy Directive as implemented nationally, in Germany by Section 25 TDDDG. Strictly necessary storage takes place without consent; everything else only with your consent.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by an external hosting provider that processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.

When you visit the website, information sent by your browser is recorded automatically: IP address, date and time, page requested, amount of data transferred, referrer URL, browser type and operating system. We need this data to deliver the website, detect errors and fend off attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Log files are deleted as soon as they are no longer required for these purposes, unless they are needed longer to investigate a security incident.

4. Cookies, local storage and consent

On your first visit, a banner asks which services you allow. We store your choice in your browser's local storage (entry alpaion_consent) for twelve months. This entry is strictly necessary and contains only your choice and its date.

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time with effect for the future by clicking in the footer of every page.

  • Essential: storing your cookie choice, security functions. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Analytics: web analytics (see section 5). Only with consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

5. Web analytics with Google Analytics 4

If you have consented to the “Analytics” category, we use Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors use the website, for example which pages they view and for how long.

Cookies are set for this purpose (including _ga and _ga_<ID>, lifetime up to two years) and usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location (country/region), device and browser information and referrer are processed. According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses.

We have concluded a data processing agreement with Google. A transfer to the USA cannot be ruled out; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply in addition. More information: policies.google.com/privacy.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). Without consent, Google Analytics is not loaded.

6. Contact form and email

If you write to us via the contact form or by email, we process your details (name, email address, optionally company, topic and message) to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract, otherwise our legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Where we use an external provider for the technical transmission of the form, this is done under a data processing agreement. We delete your data once your enquiry has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Fonts and visualisation

Fonts and the program library for the 3D visualisation are loaded directly from our own server. No connection is made to Google Fonts or any other external content delivery network.

8. Links to social networks

We link to our profiles on social networks such as LinkedIn. These are plain links, not embedded plug-ins. Data is only transferred when you click a link and open the network's page.

9. Recipients and transfers to third countries

We only share personal data where this is necessary for the purposes described, for example with hosting, form or analytics providers working on our behalf. If data is transferred to countries outside the EU, the EEA or Switzerland, we ensure an adequate level of protection, e.g. through adequacy decisions or EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Retention

We store personal data only for as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as required by statutory retention periods, such as commercial and tax law periods of up to ten years for business correspondence and invoices.

11. Your rights

Subject to the statutory requirements, you have the right to:

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR);
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR);
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR);
  • withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR);
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), e.g. the Liechtenstein Data Protection Authority, the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or the competent authority in Germany.

To exercise your rights, an email to info@alpaion.com is sufficient. We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling.

12. Data security

This website is encrypted throughout using TLS (recognisable by “https://”). We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

13. Changes

We update this privacy policy when our website, our services or the legal situation change. The version published here applies.

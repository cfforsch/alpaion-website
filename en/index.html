Approach

A structured approach in four phases. Each phase concludes with a clearly defined result and lays the foundation for the next decision.

  1. Phase 11–2 weeks

    Diagnostic

    We analyse objectives, data, processes and market context and conduct interviews with the relevant stakeholders.

    Result: baseline and target picture

  2. Phase 21–2 weeks

    Prioritisation

    We assess levers by value, effort, risk and feasibility and quantify the business case.

    Result: prioritised roadmap

  3. Phase 34–12 weeks

    Implementation

    We deliver the prioritised initiatives iteratively, validate them in live operations and optimise against defined KPIs.

    Result: live solution with measurable impact

  4. Phase 4ongoing

    Embedding

    We ensure a structured handover, enable your teams and support operations and scaling.

    Result: sustained value

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your question? Get in touch.

How are fees structured?

Strategic sparring and advisory are billed on a time-and-materials basis. After a short scoping phase, we usually offer projects at a fixed price. The initial consultation is free of charge and without obligation.

When can we expect first results?

First analyses and prototypes are typically available after two to four weeks. Each phase is designed to deliver value in its own right.

Does working with you require high data quality?

No. Most initiatives start with incomplete or heterogeneous data. We assess which data is sufficient for which purpose and improve data quality where it has the greatest effect.

How do you ensure confidentiality and data protection?

We work under non-disclosure and data processing agreements, on request exclusively within your infrastructure or with EU or Swiss hosting. We deploy AI models so that your data is not used for training.

Are you tied to specific technology vendors?

No. We advise vendor-neutrally and build on your existing system landscape wherever this makes sense.

Do you work on site or remotely?

Both. We prefer to run diagnostics, workshops and process discovery on site in the DACH region; ongoing project work is mostly remote.

What kind of organisations do you work for?

Start-ups and scale-ups, mid-sized companies, family offices and investment firms, healthcare providers and public institutions.
